# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text — including the complete enumeration of acceptable and unacceptable behavior examples, enforcement responsibilities, and scope — lives at the URL above and is the authoritative source.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the rescript-tauri community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Contributors are expected to demonstrate empathy and kindness, respect differing opinions and experiences, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

The complete list of acceptable and unacceptable behavior, together with the **Enforcement Responsibilities** of project maintainers and the **Scope** of this Code, is defined in the official Contributor Covenant 2.1 text linked above.

## Enforcement

Instances of behavior that violates this Code of Conduct may be reported to the project maintainer responsible for enforcement at:

**nagata.hbdc@gmail.com**

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow the four-tier Community Impact Guidelines defined in Contributor Covenant 2.1 — **Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban** — when determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct. The full description of each tier (impact, consequence) is available in the official text.
